Disclaimer & Safety Guidelines

Last updated:

1. Medical Disclaimer

Meditation and mindfulness practices are complementary wellness activities and are not substitutes for professional medical or psychological treatment. If you have a diagnosed medical condition, mental health disorder, or are taking medication, please consult your healthcare provider before beginning any meditation program.

2. Physical Safety

While meditation is generally safe, some practices involve prolonged sitting, specific breathing techniques, or physical postures. Please:

  • Listen to your body and stop if you experience pain or discomfort
  • Use cushions, chairs, or supports as needed for comfort
  • Avoid deep breathing exercises if you have respiratory conditions
  • Consult your doctor if you have back problems, joint issues, or cardiovascular conditions

3. Mental & Emotional Considerations

Meditation can bring up strong emotions or memories. If you have a history of:

  • Trauma, PTSD, or severe anxiety
  • Psychosis, schizophrenia, or dissociative disorders
  • Suicidal thoughts or self-harm behaviors

Please work with a qualified mental health professional alongside your meditation practice. Some intensive meditation retreats may not be suitable for individuals with certain mental health conditions.

4. Pregnancy & Special Conditions

If you are pregnant, nursing, elderly, or have mobility limitations, please inform your instructor. We can provide modified practices and seating arrangements to ensure your comfort and safety.

5. Drug & Alcohol Use

Please do not participate in meditation classes while under the influence of alcohol, recreational drugs, or medications that impair consciousness. These substances can interfere with the meditation process and may cause adverse reactions.

6. Instructor Qualifications

Our instructors are certified in various meditation traditions and wellness practices. However, they are not licensed medical professionals, psychologists, or therapists. For medical or psychological concerns, please seek appropriate professional care.

7. Personal Responsibility

You are responsible for your own wellbeing during meditation practice. PeacefulMeditation provides guidance and instruction, but ultimate responsibility for your health and safety rests with you. Please practice within your limits and seek professional help when needed.

8. Emergency Situations

If you experience a medical emergency during a class, please stop practicing immediately and seek appropriate medical attention. For online classes, have emergency contact information readily available.

9. Contact & Support

If you have questions about whether meditation is safe for your specific condition, or if you experience any adverse effects during practice, please contact us immediately:
