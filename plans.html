Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior meditation experience?

Not at all! Our Starter plan is designed specifically for complete beginners. We guide you step-by-step through the basics.

Are the classes live or pre-recorded?

We offer both! All plans include live interactive sessions with instructors, plus access to recorded sessions you can revisit anytime.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

What if I miss a live session?

No worries! All live sessions are recorded and available in your personal library within 24 hours.

Is there a refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, contact us within 14 days for a full refund.

Do you offer teacher training?

Yes! We have a comprehensive Meditation Teacher Training program. Contact us for details and enrollment dates.
