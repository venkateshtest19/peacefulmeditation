Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

PeacefulMeditation is committed to protecting your privacy. When you enroll, we collect: name, contact number, email address, gender, age, and any health conditions you voluntarily disclose. This information helps us personalize your meditation experience and ensure your safety during practice.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Process your enrollment and customize your meditation plan
  • Communicate class schedules, updates, and support
  • Ensure your safety by understanding any health considerations
  • Send wellness tips and program-related notifications
  • Improve our services based on community feedback

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Health Information Privacy

Any health conditions or concerns you disclose are treated with strict confidentiality. This information is used only to ensure your meditation practice is safe and appropriate for your needs. We do not share health data with instructors without your explicit consent.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. All data transmissions are encrypted using SSL technology. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience and basic analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Google Analytics may collect anonymous usage data.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services for payment processing, email delivery, or video conferencing. These partners are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for specified purposes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Opt out of non-essential communications at any time
  • Withdraw consent for health data usage

8. Children's Privacy

Our family plans include children's meditation classes. For participants under 18, we require parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental approval.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy occasionally.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

Address: 7th Street, 40 E 7th St, New York, NY 10003, USA

Your privacy and trust are sacred to us. We honor your commitment to sharing your journey with us.
